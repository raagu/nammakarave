GEOGRAPHIC DATA
Contains administrative boundary data from the Local Government Directory (LGD),
Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Government of India, derived from the
ramSeraph/indian_admin_boundaries compilation and the india-village-finder
Karnataka district dataset, used under the Government Open Data License – India.
Dataset snapshot: 25 September 2026. Retrieved: 9 November 2026.
Source: https://github.com/mchittineni/india-village-finder/blob/main/karnataka/web/data/districts.geojson
Upstream: https://github.com/ramSeraph/indian_admin_boundaries
Licence: https://data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india
Modifications: Mercator projection, display simplification, normalized bilingual
labels, and interior representative markers. Markers identify districts rather
than office addresses. The map is for the website directory, not legal surveying.
